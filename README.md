# 🤖 AI Sales Intelligence Assistant

> **An AI-powered Sales Intelligence & Customer Email Automation System**

Developed by **AI Weekline**

---

## Overview

AI Sales Intelligence Assistant is an enterprise-ready AI automation solution that analyzes customer emails, extracts business intelligence, structures important sales information, and generates intelligent AI-powered responses automatically.

The solution combines **Google Gemini**, **Make.com**, **Gmail**, and **Google Sheets** to automate repetitive sales operations while improving response quality and customer engagement.

---

## Key Features

- 📧 Automatic Gmail Monitoring
- 🤖 AI Email Analysis using Google Gemini
- 🧠 Customer Intent Detection
- 😊 Sentiment Analysis
- 🎯 AI Lead Scoring
- 📋 Structured Customer Data Extraction
- ✉️ Intelligent Email Reply Generation
- 📊 Google Sheets Data Storage
- ⚡ Fully Automated Workflow
- ☁️ Cloud-Based Architecture
