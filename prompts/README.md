# AI Sales Intelligence Assistant - Prompt Library

This directory contains the AI prompts used by the AI Sales Intelligence Assistant developed by AI Weekline.

## Purpose

The prompts instruct Google Gemini to:

- Analyze customer emails
- Extract key business information
- Identify customer intent
- Detect action items
- Generate professional email replies
- Structure information for Google Sheets

---

## Prompt Categories

### Email Analysis Prompt

Extracts:

- Customer Name
- Company
- Email Subject
- Intent
- Priority
- Products Mentioned
- Action Items
- Summary

---

### Email Reply Prompt

Generates:

- Professional responses
- Clear business communication
- Polite acknowledgements
- Follow-up recommendations

---

### Data Structuring Prompt

Formats AI output into a structured format suitable for Google Sheets.
